Our Story: Empowering Nigerian Students

Founded by Tutor Rofiu, ExamGuideNG was born from a passion to make quality exam preparation accessible to every Nigerian student.

Meet Tutor Rofiu

Abdurrofi Animashaun (Tutor Rofiu) is a passionate educator with over 8 years of experience preparing Nigerian students for WAEC, NECO, and JAMB examinations. His journey began as a private tutor in Lagos, where he noticed how many students struggled due to lack of access to quality study materials.

With a degree in Education from the University of Lagos and multiple teaching certifications, Tutor Rofiu combines pedagogical expertise with practical exam strategies that have helped thousands of students achieve academic success.

"My greatest joy is seeing students who once feared mathematics solve complex problems with confidence," he often says.

How ExamGuideNG Began

In 2015, after years of tutoring students individually, Tutor Rofiu recognized a systemic problem - most students couldn't afford private tutoring, and existing study materials were either outdated or too expensive.

He started by compiling past questions and solutions for his students, sharing them freely. The demand grew exponentially, and in 2017, ExamGuideNG was officially launched as a digital platform to reach more students across Nigeria.

What began as a simple WhatsApp study group has now grown into Nigeria's most comprehensive exam preparation platform, serving over 500,000 students annually with verified past questions, video lessons, and personalized tutoring.
